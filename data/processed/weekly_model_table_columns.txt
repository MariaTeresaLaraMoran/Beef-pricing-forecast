Columns in weekly_model_table.csv
========================================

week                 week ending Sunday (index)
us_<cut>             weekly USDA boxed beef price, USD/cwt
log_<series>         natural log of the series
ret_<series>         weekly log return (first difference of the log)
ret_<series>_lagN    that return, N weeks earlier
us_steer_dressed     5-Area fed steer price, USD/cwt CARCASS
                     (LM_CT150) - the packer's input cost
us_steer_live        the same cattle quoted per cwt LIVE weight
margin_<grade>_usd   cutout minus dressed steer price, USD/cwt:
                     the gross packer margin, a supply-side driver
margin_<grade>_log   the same as a log ratio, scale-free
margin_<grade>_log_change   weekly change in that log margin
us_steer_*_head      weekly NEGOTIATED head count, 5-Area:
                     throughput, not total federal slaughter
usd_index            FRED DTWEXBGS, broad trade-weighted dollar
initial_claims       FRED ICSA, weekly initial jobless claims
season_sin, season_cos   week of year as a smooth annual cycle
target_<cut>_h4         log return over the NEXT 4 weeks (the only forward-looking columns)
target_<cut>_h12         log return over the NEXT 12 weeks (the only forward-looking columns)
